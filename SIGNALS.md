# Who you prospect, the signals, and their points

Status: EXAMPLE. Your agent asks you and replaces this line with "Status: yours" once the file is yours.

## Who you sell to

VPs, heads and directors of Sales, Sales Development, Sales Operations and Revenue Operations, and SDR/BDR managers, in the United States, the United Kingdom, France and Germany.

**Not a fit:** consultants, advisors, fractional and independent roles; students and job seekers.

## The signal searches

One Sales Navigator search per signal, each on the buyers above: the buyer search plus one filter.

| Signal | Sales Navigator filter | Why it means "now" |
|---|---|---|
| `changed_jobs` | Changed jobs in the last 90 days | A new leader rebuilds the stack and the process in the first months. |
| `viewed_profile` | Viewed your profile recently | They looked at you. Your own first-party signal. |
| `follows_company` | Following your company | They chose to follow your news. |
| `posted` | Posted on LinkedIn in the last 30 days | They are active, and you have something to reply to. |

`score.py` also reads two signals from each person's own data: **under 3 months in role**, and **company growth of 30% or more in a year** (a hiring surge).

## Points

Start here, then audit your closed deals: raise the signals that show up in wins, lower the ones that show up in losses. A person found by several searches gets the points of each.

| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| `changed_jobs` | 9 |
| `viewed_profile` | 8 |
| `fast_growth` | 7 |
| `fit` | 5 |
| `follows_company` | 4 |
| `posted` | 3 |
| `new_in_role` | 3 |

`fit` is what every person gets for being in the buyer search.
